Privacy Notice

Information about personal data submitted through this website’s contact form, under Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Data controller

The data controller is Yuri Scarzelletti, who can be contacted at scarzellettiyuri@hotmail.it. You can use the same address for privacy-related requests.

Personal data collected

When you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address and message, together with any other personal information you voluntarily include. The technical service provider may also process connection and security information, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. Please avoid submitting sensitive or unnecessary personal information.

Purposes and lawful bases

The data are used to receive, manage and reply to professional enquiries and other messages you voluntarily send. The lawful basis is the controller’s legitimate interest in handling correspondence (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, where an enquiry concerns a potential contractual relationship, taking pre-contractual steps at your request (Article 6(1)(b)). The form is not used to enrol you automatically in newsletters or marketing.

Whether providing data is required

Submitting the form is optional. A name, email address and message are required in order to receive and respond to your enquiry; without them, the form cannot be submitted. You can also contact the email address shown on the website directly.

Service providers and recipients

The form is handled by Formspree, Inc., which receives and forwards messages. Submissions may be stored in the Formspree account and forwarded to the configured email inbox, whose provider supplies technical email services. See Formspree’s privacy policy for further information.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Formspree states that it uses infrastructure in the United States and relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) when acting as a data processor. See Formspree’s security page for current details. Any further transfers depend on the email services actually configured.

Retention

Messages are retained only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any related professional correspondence, and longer only where required by law or to protect legal rights. Technical retention of copies in Formspree depends on the account plan and settings; email copies are also subject to the mailbox’s data-management procedures.

Your rights

Where applicable under the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact scarzellettiyuri@hotmail.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Italian Data Protection Authority.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if contact-handling arrangements or service providers change. The revision date is shown on this page.
